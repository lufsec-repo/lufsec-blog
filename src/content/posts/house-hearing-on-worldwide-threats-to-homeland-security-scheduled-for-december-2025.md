## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **House Hearing on Worldwide Threats to Homeland Security Scheduled for December 2025** — *tl;dr:* The House Committee on Homeland Security will hold a hearing titled 'Worldwide Threats to the Homeland' on December 11, 2025, at 10:00 AM ET in the Cannon House Office Building. Key witnesses include DHS Secretary Kristi Noem and FBI Operations Director Michael Glasheen. The hearing aims to address various threats facing the U.S., including terrorism and cybersecurity challenges. It will be livestreamed for public access, emphasizing transparency and engagement with citizens. Stakeholders in national security, law enforcement, and emergency management are encouraged to follow the proceedings for insights on evolving threats.

5. **Troubleshooting Exchange Online PowerShell Connection Issues** — *tl;dr:* Users experiencing timeouts or dropped sessions while connecting to Exchange Online PowerShell can take several steps to resolve the issue. Ensure you are using the latest ExchangeOnlineManagement module and verify your PowerShell execution policy is set to RemoteSigned. Check for any network configurations, such as firewalls or proxies, that may interfere with the connection. Additionally, consider disabling Windows Authentication Manager (WAM) temporarily to see if it alleviates the problem. Collect diagnostic logs for further insights into the connection issues.
