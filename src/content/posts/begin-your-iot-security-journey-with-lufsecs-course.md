However, with this expansion comes a growing concern: the security of these devices. Through real-world scenarios and case studies, you’ll gain invaluable insights into the cyber-attacks specifically targeting IoT devices.

The course moves beyond merely identifying problems, offering actionable solutions to tackle IoT-specific challenges head-on. By following industry best practices and implementing the recommended countermeasures, you are paving the way towards a more secure IoT environment.
