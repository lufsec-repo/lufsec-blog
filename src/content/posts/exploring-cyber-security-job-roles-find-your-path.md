#### Exploring Cyber Security Job Roles - Find Your Path

The field of cyber security is vast and diverse, offering a wide range of job roles to suit different interests and skill sets. Whether you're a problem solver, a tech enthusiast, or someone who enjoys working with people, there's a role in cyber security for you. In this post, we delve into Chapter 2 of our comprehensive eBook, "Starting Your Cyber Security Career: Building a Successful Career in Cyber Security," and explore the various cyber security job roles available.

### Why Understanding Cyber Security Job Roles is Crucial

Knowing the different job roles in cyber security helps you identify your career path and focus your efforts on the necessary skills and certifications. Each role comes with its own set of responsibilities, required skills, and salary expectations. By understanding these roles, you can make informed decisions about your education and career trajectory.

### Common Cyber Security Job Roles

Here are some of the most common and in-demand cyber security job roles:

**1. Security Analyst**

**Responsibilities:**

- Monitor networks and systems for security breaches.
- Investigate and respond to security incidents.
- Implement and maintain security measures and tools.
- Conduct vulnerability assessments and penetration testing.
- Prepare reports and documentation on security status and incidents.

**Skills Required:**

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Knowledge of networking and operating systems.
- Familiarity with security tools and technologies (e.g., SIEM, IDS/IPS).
- Understanding of threat modeling and risk assessment techniques.

**Salary Expectations:**

- Entry-Level: $50,000 - $70,000
- Mid-Level: $70,000 - $90,000
- Senior-Level: $90,000 - $120,000

**2. Security Engineer**

**Responsibilities:**

- Design and implement secure network solutions.
- Develop and enforce security policies and procedures.
- Maintain and update security systems, such as firewalls and intrusion detection systems.
- Perform regular security audits and assessments.
- Collaborate with IT and development teams to secure applications and infrastructure.

**Skills Required:**

- Strong technical background in networking and system administration.
- Experience with security technologies (e.g., firewalls, VPNs, IDS/IPS).
- Knowledge of scripting and automation tools.
- Understanding of secure coding practices and software development life cycle (SDLC).

**Salary Expectations:**

- Entry-Level: $70,000 - $90,000
- Mid-Level: $90,000 - $110,000
- Senior-Level: $110,000 - $140,000

**3. Security Architect**

**Responsibilities:**

- Develop and design security architecture for systems and networks.
- Ensure compliance with security standards and best practices.
- Assess and mitigate risks associated with new and existing systems.
- Create and maintain security policies, procedures, and guidelines.
- Provide strategic guidance on security initiatives and projects.

**Skills Required:**

- Extensive knowledge of security principles and frameworks.
- Experience in designing and implementing security solutions.
- Strong understanding of enterprise architecture and cloud security.
- Ability to communicate security concepts to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

**Salary Expectations:**

- Entry-Level: $90,000 - $110,000
- Mid-Level: $110,000 - $140,000
- Senior-Level: $140,000 - $180,000

**4. Penetration Tester (Ethical Hacker)**

**Responsibilities:**

- Conduct simulated attacks on systems and networks to identify vulnerabilities.
- Develop and execute penetration testing plans and methodologies.
- Report findings and provide recommendations for remediation.
- Stay updated on the latest security threats and techniques.
- Collaborate with security teams to improve defenses.

**Skills Required:**

- Proficiency in various penetration testing tools and techniques.
- Strong understanding of networking, operating systems, and web applications.
- Knowledge of scripting languages (e.g., Python, Bash).
- Ability to think like an attacker and identify creative exploitation methods.

**Salary Expectations:**

- Entry-Level: $70,000 - $90,000
- Mid-Level: $90,000 - $120,000
- Senior-Level: $120,000 - $150,000

**5. Incident Responder**

**Responsibilities:**

- Investigate and respond to security incidents and breaches.
- Perform forensic analysis to determine the cause and extent of incidents.
- Develop and implement incident response plans and procedures.
- Coordinate with other teams and stakeholders during incidents.
- Document incidents and lessons learned to improve future response efforts.

**Skills Required:**

- Strong analytical and investigative skills.
- Experience with forensic tools and techniques.
- Knowledge of incident response frameworks and best practices.
- Ability to remain calm and effective under pressure.

**Salary Expectations:**

- Entry-Level: $60,000 - $80,000
- Mid-Level: $80,000 - $100,000
- Senior-Level: $100,000 - $130,000

### Other Cyber Security Job Roles

In addition to the roles mentioned above, there are many other specialized positions in the field of cyber security, including:

**Security Consultant:**

- Provides expert advice to clients on security measures, policies, and best practices.

**Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):**

- Oversees the organization’s overall security strategy and ensures compliance with security regulations.

**Cryptographer:**

- Designs and implements cryptographic algorithms and systems.

**Forensic Expert:**

- Conducts digital forensic investigations to uncover evidence of cyber crimes.
