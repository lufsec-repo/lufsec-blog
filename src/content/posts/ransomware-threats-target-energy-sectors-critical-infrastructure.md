*Thursday, March 26, 2026*

## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Ransomware Threats Target Energy Sector's Critical Infrastructure** — *tl;dr:* The energy sector continues to face significant ransomware threats, impacting critical infrastructure and raising concerns about national security. Recent attacks highlight vulnerabilities in systems that support essential services, urging organizations to adopt robust cybersecurity measures. Stakeholders in the energy industry must prioritize threat intelligence and incident response strategies to mitigate risks. Implementing advanced security solutions and employee training can enhance resilience against evolving cyber threats, ensuring the protection of vital resources and services.  
   ↪ <https://cyble.com/blog/energy-sector-ransomware-attack-report/>
2. **FBI Warns of Iranian Hacking Group Targeting Dissidents** — *tl;dr:* The FBI has issued a warning about the Iranian hacking group Handala, which has been actively targeting opponents of the Iranian regime since 2023. This group is linked to hack-and-leak operations aimed at discrediting dissidents. Organizations and individuals opposing the Iranian government are advised to enhance their cybersecurity measures, including using strong authentication methods and monitoring for suspicious activity. The warning highlights the ongoing threat posed by state-sponsored cyber actors and the need for vigilance in protecting sensitive information.  
   ↪ <https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/data-breaches/>
3. **BridgePay Ransomware Attack: Analyzing the Impact and Response** — *tl;dr:* The recent ransomware attack on BridgePay has highlighted significant vulnerabilities in cybersecurity protocols affecting financial transaction services. This incident underscores the critical need for robust cybersecurity measures, including regular security audits and employee training on phishing awareness. Organizations in the fintech sector must prioritize compliance with standards like ISO 27001 and implement comprehensive incident response plans to mitigate risks. The attack serves as a reminder for all businesses to strengthen their defenses against evolving cyber threats and ensure business continuity through effective resilience strategies.  
   ↪ <https://www.isms.online/cyber-security/the-resilience-factor-breaking-down-the-bridgepay-ransomware-attack/>
4. **CVE-2026-4329: Stored XSS Vulnerability in Blackhole for Bad Bots Plugin** — *tl;dr:* The Blackhole for Bad Bots plugin for WordPress, up to version 3.8, is vulnerable to a Stored Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) attack via the User-Agent HTTP header. This vulnerability arises from inadequate input sanitization and output escaping, allowing unauthenticated attackers to inject malicious scripts that execute when an administrator views the log page. Website administrators using this plugin should update to the latest version immediately to mitigate potential security risks and safeguard against unauthorized script execution.  
   ↪ <https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-4329>
5. **Data Breaches Affect Millions: Navia, North Texas Behavioral Health, OSI Systems** — *tl;dr:* This week, significant data breaches were reported affecting Navia Benefit Solutions, North Texas Behavioral Health Authority, and OSI Systems. Navia exposed PII of nearly 2.7 million individuals, including Social Security numbers and health plan information. North Texas Behavioral Health reported a breach impacting 284,525 victims, compromising sensitive medical and personal data. OSI Systems suffered a ransomware attack, affecting 4,900 individuals with stolen financial and employment records. Affected individuals should take immediate action to secure their information and may be eligible for compensation through legal avenues.  
   ↪ <https://www.forthepeople.com/blog/data-breach-brief-week-march-25th-2026/>
