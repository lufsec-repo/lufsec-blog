Customers can make orders for online cybersecurity courses and other services LufSec provides with bitcoin, bitcoin cash, Dai, Ethereum, Litecoin, and USD coin.

Today LufSec is trying to go hand in hand with technological innovation, using what technology offers to support our customer's demand. There were a number of customers that asked for that type of payment method and it makes total sense for the type of business that we are. Our main customers are cybersecurity professionals, students, and  IT professionals related.
