### Why Security Awareness Training is Essential for Businesses

Cybersecurity isn’t just an IT problem—it’s a **company-wide responsibility**. Organizations that fail to implement proper security awareness training risk exposing themselves to phishing scams, social engineering attacks, weak password practices, and insider threats. Many **compliance regulations and security frameworks** now mandate that organizations conduct regular employee training to ensure their workforce can effectively handle security threats.

This is where our **Security Awareness Training** comes in. This training is designed to provide corporate users and IT security teams with **essential cybersecurity knowledge**, helping them recognize threats and respond appropriately, ultimately **reducing the risk of cyber incidents**.

### What This Training Covers

This **free** Security Awareness Training covers fundamental cybersecurity principles that every employee should understand. Participants will learn:

✅ **How to identify phishing emails** and avoid becoming a victim of social engineering scams.  
✅ **Best practices for password security** and why **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is critical.  
✅ **Why leaving a workstation unlocked** is a security risk and how to mitigate unauthorized access.  
✅ **The dangers of plugging in unknown USB drives** and how external devices can be compromised.  
✅ **How to recognize and report security incidents** to IT security teams.
