## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Healthcare Data Breaches Expose Patient Info Amid Rising Supply Chain Ransomware Risks** — *tl;dr:* Recent healthcare data breaches, including incidents at the University of Nebraska Medical Center and Radiology Associates of Richmond, have compromised sensitive patient information for hundreds of thousands. A GitHub breach linked to a malicious VS Code script highlights the vulnerabilities in developer tools, while ransomware attacks on vendors like Foxconn demonstrate the expanding threat landscape. As third-party systems increasingly expose organizations to risk, healthcare providers must enhance their cybersecurity measures, including microsegmentation and vendor risk management, to protect patient data and maintain operational integrity.  
   ↪ <https://colortokens.com/blogs/healthcare-data-breaches-supply-chain-ransomware-protection>
2. **Carnival Data Breach Affects Nearly 6 Million Customers** — *tl;dr:* Carnival Corporation has confirmed a data breach impacting approximately 6 million individuals, following an incident where an employee was manipulated into granting access to the company's IT systems. The breach, which occurred on April 14, 2026, involved the theft of personal data, including names, email addresses, dates of birth, and membership status. The extortion group ShinyHunters claimed responsibility, highlighting the high value of cruise passenger data for identity theft and fraud. Affected individuals are urged to remain vigilant against phishing attempts and are being offered a 24-month credit monitoring service.  
   ↪ <https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/data-breaches/2026/05/carnival-confirms-data-breach-impacting-nearly-6-million>
3. **Multiple Data Breaches Reported on May 28, 2026, Affecting Various Companies** — *tl;dr:* On May 28, 2026, several data breaches were reported, impacting various organizations, including Andalusia Country Club, Base Spa, and Delbrook Capital Advisors. The breaches involved different threat actors such as Akira, SpaceBears, and DragonForce. Companies across sectors, including telecommunications, law, and energy, have been affected, potentially compromising sensitive customer and corporate data. Organizations are advised to enhance their cybersecurity measures, conduct thorough audits, and monitor for unauthorized access to mitigate risks associated with these breaches.  
   ↪ <https://www.breachsense.com/breaches>
4. **Carnival Cruise Data Breach Affects Nearly 6 Million Customers** — *tl;dr:* Carnival Cruise has confirmed a data breach impacting approximately 6 million individuals, following an attack by the ShinyHunters cybercrime group. The breach, which occurred on April 10, 2026, involved unauthorized access to employee accounts through social engineering tactics. Affected data includes names, birth dates, email addresses, and loyalty program details. Carnival has begun notifying customers and is working with security experts to enhance their defenses. Customers are advised to monitor their accounts and consider identity protection services to mitigate potential risks from the exposed information.  
   ↪ <https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/carnival-cruise-confirms-data-breach-affecting-nearly-6-million-people>
5. **Motility Software Solutions Settles Data Breach for $4.95 Million** — *tl;dr:* Motility Software Solutions has agreed to a $4.95 million settlement following a data breach in August 2025 that compromised the personal information of approximately 760,797 individuals. Affected individuals can claim up to $5,000 for documented losses related to fraud or identity theft, or a $75 alternate cash payment, along with two years of free credit monitoring. Claims must be submitted by August 7, 2026. The settlement aims to address allegations of inadequate protection of private information, though Motility denies any wrongdoing.  
   ↪ <https://www.claimdepot.com/settlements/motility-data-breach-litigation>
