## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **2026 Financial Services Cybersecurity Report: Vendor CVEs Surge Amid Ransomware Rise** — *tl;dr:* The 2026 Financial Services Cybersecurity Report reveals a 30% increase in ransomware incidents, rising from 156 to 202 in 2025, with Q1 2026 already showing a 76% spike. The report highlights a significant rise in critical vulnerabilities among vendors, with those carrying CVSS 9+ vulnerabilities increasing from 15 to 73 in just one year. Financial institutions are urged to reassess their vendor risk management strategies, as 54% of core vendors now carry actively exploited vulnerabilities. The evolving threat landscape necessitates heightened vigilance and proactive measures to mitigate risks associated with third-party dependencies.  
   ↪ <https://blackkite.com/reports/2026-financial-services-report>
2. **Surge in Tax Phishing Campaigns Hits Australia Amid Cybercrime Concerns** — *tl;dr:* Australia is experiencing a significant increase in tax phishing campaigns, with over 100 reported incidents. Cybercriminals are targeting individuals and businesses during the tax season, exploiting vulnerabilities to steal sensitive information. This surge in cybercrime highlights the need for heightened awareness and vigilance among Australians. Individuals are advised to verify the authenticity of emails and messages claiming to be from tax authorities and to report any suspicious activity. As cyber threats evolve, staying informed and adopting robust security practices is crucial to protect personal and financial data.  
   ↪ <https://cybersecurityventures.com>
3. **Ultrahuman Reports Data Breach Affecting Customers' Wellness Data** — *tl;dr:* Ultrahuman, a wearable health tech startup, announced that hackers accessed customers' wellness data by stealing an employee's credentials through malware. The breach, detected on March 27, impacted approximately 0.1% of its 700,000 monthly active users, equating to around 700 customers. While no passwords, payment information, or devices were compromised, the incident underscores the risks associated with storing sensitive health data. Affected users were notified via email, and Ultrahuman is cooperating with regulators while investigating the breach. Customers are advised to monitor their accounts for unusual activity.  
   ↪ <https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/ultrahuman-says-hackers-accessed-customers-wellness-data-via-internal-tool>
4. **EMM Loans Settles Class Action Over February 2024 Data Breach** — *tl;dr:* EMM Loans, LLC has settled a class action lawsuit related to a data breach that occurred in February 2024, affecting approximately 2,313 individuals. The settlement offers cash compensation of up to $4,000 for unreimbursed losses and up to $50 for those opting for an alternative cash payment. Additionally, affected individuals can receive two years of credit monitoring services. Class members must submit claims by August 17, 2026, to be eligible for compensation. The final approval hearing for the settlement is scheduled for August 24, 2026.  
   ↪ <https://www.classaction.org/news/emm-loans-settlement-ends-class-action-lawsuit-over-february-2024-data-breach>
5. **Middletown Data Breach Exposes Social Security Numbers and Personal Information** — *tl;dr:* A cybersecurity incident in Middletown, Ohio, from July to August 2025, resulted in the exposure of personal information, including names, addresses, and Social Security numbers. City officials announced on May 18, 2026, that while there is no evidence of fraud, affected individuals have been notified as a precaution. Those impacted were provided with best practices for protecting their information and offered complimentary credit monitoring services. Residents are advised to review health insurance statements for any discrepancies to safeguard against potential identity theft.  
   ↪ <https://www.wlwt.com/article/middletown-social-security-numbers-personal-info-data-breach/71487732>
