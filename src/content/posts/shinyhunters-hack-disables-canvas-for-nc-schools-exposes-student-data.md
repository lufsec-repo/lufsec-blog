## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **ShinyHunters Hack Disables Canvas for NC Schools, Exposes Student Data** — *tl;dr:* The hacker group ShinyHunters has claimed responsibility for a data breach of Instructure, the company managing Canvas, disrupting access for nearly 9,000 schools, including several in North Carolina. The breach, which affects personal data such as names and email addresses of students, staff, and parents, has led to immediate action from school districts like Wake County and Durham, which have disabled Canvas access. Schools are urged to monitor communications for updates and assess the potential impact on personal information while preparing alternative instructional methods during this critical end-of-year period.  
   ↪ <https://www.wral.com/news/education/canvas-shinyhunters-ransom-instructure-hack-data-breach-may-2026/>
2. **Massive Ransomware Attack Affects Michigan Schools via Instructure's Canvas** — *tl;dr:* A significant ransomware attack has impacted multiple educational institutions in Michigan, including the University of Michigan and Wayne State University, following a breach at Instructure, the company behind the Canvas learning management system. The hacker group 'ShinyHunters' claims to have affected 8,950 entities nationwide, threatening to leak data of approximately 275 million students. Instructure has confirmed that Canvas is operational but advises affected institutions to implement security best practices, such as enforcing multi-factor authentication and reviewing admin access. Students have reported receiving ransom messages while trying to access services.  
   ↪ <https://www.fox2detroit.com/news/cyberattack-targets-university-michigan-thousands-other-schools-massive-breach>
3. **Canvas Learning Platform Breached, Exposing Data of Millions of Students and Teachers** — *tl;dr:* Instructure's Canvas learning management system has suffered a significant data breach, potentially exposing the personal information of millions of students and educators. The criminal group ShinyHunters claims to have stolen 275 million records, including names, email addresses, and private messages. While passwords and financial data appear safe, the breach raises concerns due to the sensitivity of the disclosed messages. Affected institutions are advised to monitor for unusual activity and be cautious of phishing attempts. Instructure is currently investigating the breach with external cybersecurity experts and law enforcement.  
   ↪ <https://www.abc10.com/article/news/nation-world/canvas-hack-shinyhunters-schools-students-teachers-data-exposed/507-0f3f5973-3d68-45af-b309-666561b2bd87>
4. **Canvas Data Breach Disrupts Schools Nationwide; University of Illinois Postpones Exams** — *tl;dr:* A significant data breach affecting Canvas, the learning management system by Instructure, has disrupted educational institutions across the U.S., including the University of Illinois and Illinois State University. Hackers, identifying themselves as 'ShinyHunters,' exploited vulnerabilities in Free-For-Teacher accounts, threatening to expose stolen information unless a ransom is paid. While sensitive data like Social Security numbers remain secure, names, email addresses, and student IDs have been compromised. Affected users are advised to monitor their accounts closely and remain vigilant against phishing attempts as institutions assess the breach's impact.  
   ↪ <https://abc7chicago.com/post/canvas-hacked-data-breach-affects-schools-nationwide-including-university-illinois-isu/19060406/>
5. **Canvas Cybersecurity Breach Affects Millions of Students, Alerts Issued** — *tl;dr:* A significant cybersecurity breach involving Canvas, the online education platform by Instructure, has potentially exposed data for millions of students across various institutions. The hacking group 'ShinyHunters' claims to have accessed sensitive information and threatens to leak it unless contacted by May 12, 2026. Colleges in the Monterey Bay area, including California State University Monterey Bay and Monterey Peninsula College, are responding to the incident, advising students to remain vigilant against phishing attempts. While Canvas remains operational, affected institutions are monitoring the situation and taking steps to secure their systems.  
   ↪ <https://www.ksbw.com/article/canvas-cybersecurity-attack-millions-prompts-alerts-monterey-bay-colleges/71245595>
