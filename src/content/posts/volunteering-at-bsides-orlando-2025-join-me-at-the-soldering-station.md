This Saturday, I’ll be participating in [**BSides Orlando 2025**](https://bsidesorlando.org/), one of the most exciting community-driven security conferences in Florida. I’m thrilled to share that I’ll be **volunteering at the soldering station**, helping attendees learn, build, and hack with hands-on electronics.

## Why BSides?

BSides conferences are special. They bring together hackers, researchers, and learners from all backgrounds to share knowledge, collaborate, and inspire the next generation of security professionals. Unlike massive industry events, BSides has a unique grassroots energy—where you can meet people face-to-face, ask questions, and get your hands dirty with real projects.

## The Soldering Station

If you’re attending, stop by the soldering station! Whether it’s your first time holding a soldering iron or you’re already comfortable with hardware hacking, it’s an excellent opportunity to sharpen your skills and walk away with something you built yourself.

I’ll be there volunteering, answering questions, and sharing my experience from years of working in cybersecurity.
