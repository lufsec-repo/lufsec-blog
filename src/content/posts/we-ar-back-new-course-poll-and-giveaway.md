### 2. Spotlight on Car Hacking 101

My latest offering, **Car Hacking 101: From Keyfob Cracking to Full System Exploitation**, is designed to introduce you to the key concepts needed to understand and potentially exploit the technologies inside today’s vehicles. The course covers:

1. **Keyfob Cracking** – Learn how modern remote key systems can be compromised and what measures can protect them.
2. **CAN Bus Basics** – Get an overview of the Controller Area Network protocol, integral to automotive networking.
3. **Vulnerability Identification** – Discover where common weaknesses lie in automotive software, hardware, and firmware.
4. **Mitigation Strategies** – Equip yourself with the best practices for safeguarding vehicles against real-world attacks.

By the end of this course, you’ll have a comprehensive view of how a car’s digital ecosystem functions, the vulnerabilities that lurk within, and the defensive tactics that can neutralize these threats. If you’re eager to start, head to my course page at [lufsec.com/products](https://lufsec.com/products) to learn more.
