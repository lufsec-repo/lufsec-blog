   ↪ <https://imnews.imbc.com/news/2025/econo/article/6783846_36737.html>
3. **Coupang Faces Major Data Breach: 33 Million Customer Records Stolen by Ex-Employee** — *tl;dr:* Coupang, South Korea's largest e-commerce platform, has suffered a significant data breach, with 33 million customer records stolen by a former employee from China. The breach went undetected for five months and included sensitive information such as personal details, order history, and even electronic lock codes for homes. This incident raises serious national security concerns and has led to government investigations and potential legal actions against the company. Affected customers are advised to monitor their accounts and change passwords to mitigate risks.  
   ↪ <https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183025/post/202512100035/>
4. **Federal Reserve to Announce Interest Rate Cut Decision at December Meeting** — *tl;dr:* The Federal Reserve is set to announce its interest rate decision during its final meeting of 2025, with a strong likelihood of a quarter-point cut. This decision comes amid a challenging economic landscape marked by stubborn inflation and a rocky job market, where job seekers outnumber vacancies. Chair Jerome Powell has indicated that the decision is not predetermined, but current market indicators suggest a 90% chance of a rate reduction. If implemented, this cut could lead to lower borrowing costs for consumers and businesses, potentially stimulating economic activity in the new year.  
   ↪ <https://www.businessinsider.com/fed-rate-cut-decision-december-meeting-live-updates-2025-12>
5. **Upcoming U.S. Naval Institute Events: 2025-2026 Schedule** — *tl;dr:* The U.S. Naval Institute has announced a series of upcoming events, including the Defense Forum Washington on December 10, 2025, focusing on U.S. shipbuilding strategies. Additionally, the 2026 U.S. Naval Institute Member Event is scheduled for February 11, 2026, at The Ultimate Skybox in San Diego. The 36th annual WEST Conference will also take place from February 10-12, 2026, featuring discussions on defense and technology. These events offer valuable networking and learning opportunities for defense professionals and members of the naval community. Interested participants should register early to secure their spots.
