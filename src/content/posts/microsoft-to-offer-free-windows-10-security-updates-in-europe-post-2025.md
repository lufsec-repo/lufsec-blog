*Monday, January 26, 2026*

## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Microsoft to Offer Free Windows 10 Security Updates in Europe Post-2025** — *tl;dr:* In response to regulatory pressure, Microsoft has announced that it will provide free security updates for Windows 10 in Europe after 2025, while continuing to charge users in other regions. This decision aims to enhance security for European users amid increasing cybersecurity threats. Organizations relying on Windows 10 in Europe should prepare for these updates to ensure their systems remain secure without incurring additional costs. Meanwhile, users outside Europe should consider upgrading to Windows 11 to maintain support and security.  
   ↪ <https://insightsintothings.com/tech-headlines/>
2. **Fortinet Firewalls Targeted by Exploited Authentication Bypass Vulnerability** — *tl;dr:* Fortinet is addressing a critical authentication bypass vulnerability in its FortiCloud SSO, which has been exploited even in fully patched firewalls. The company confirmed reports of fresh exploitation activity, indicating a new attack vector. Organizations using Fortinet firewalls should ensure they are running the latest updates and monitor their systems for unusual activity. This incident highlights the ongoing challenge of cybersecurity, where patched systems can still be vulnerable, emphasizing the need for continuous vigilance and proactive security measures.  
   ↪ <https://thehackernews.com/>
3. **Understanding Cybersecurity: Threats, Trends, and Importance** — *tl;dr:* Cybersecurity is essential for protecting individuals and organizations from cyberattacks, including ransomware, phishing, and data theft. With the rise of sophisticated threats powered by AI and the increasing costs of data breaches—averaging $4.88 million—businesses must prioritize their cybersecurity strategies. The evolving IT landscape, including cloud computing and remote work, presents new vulnerabilities that cybercriminals exploit. Organizations should invest in robust cybersecurity measures, including threat detection, incident response, and employee training, to safeguard their data and systems against emerging threats.  
   ↪ <https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/cybersecurity>
4. **Andy Frain Services Notifies 100,000 of Ransomware Data Breach** — *tl;dr:* Andy Frain Services has informed over 100,000 individuals that their personal information was compromised in a ransomware attack attributed to the Black Basta group in October 2024. The breach involved the theft of 750 GB of data, although specific details about the compromised information remain undisclosed. Experts have raised concerns about the seven-month delay in notification, emphasizing the need for transparency regarding the breach's cause and mitigation strategies. Organizations are urged to enhance their cybersecurity measures to prevent similar incidents and protect sensitive data.  
   ↪ <https://nationalcybersecurity.com/andy-frain-notifies-100000-after-major-ransomware-breach-hacking-cybersecurity-infosec-comptia-pentest-ransomware/>
5. **Kroll Reports 356% Surge in CVE and Zero-Day Cyber Attacks in Q4 2021** — *tl;dr:* Kroll's latest report reveals a staggering 356% increase in cyber attacks exploiting common vulnerabilities and exposures (CVEs) and zero-day vulnerabilities in Q4 2021 compared to the previous quarter. This alarming trend poses significant risks to organizations, as many may unknowingly run vulnerable software. Compliance professionals are urged to implement robust processes for monitoring and patching software vulnerabilities, ensuring timely updates, and maintaining a comprehensive threat intelligence system. Organizations should consider engaging virtual Chief Information Security Officers (V-CISOs) for cost-effective cybersecurity solutions and continuous monitoring to mitigate these rising threats.  
   ↪ <https://www.corporatecomplianceinsights.com/common-and-zero-day-vulnerability-cyber-incidents-tripled-in-q4-2021/>
