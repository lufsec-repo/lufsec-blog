## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **FBI Confirms Major Cyber Hack of Its Systems** — *tl;dr:* The FBI has confirmed a significant cyber breach affecting its systems, raising concerns about the security of sensitive data. The breach highlights vulnerabilities within federal cybersecurity measures and may impact ongoing investigations and national security operations. Organizations should review their cybersecurity protocols, enhance monitoring systems, and ensure compliance with data protection regulations to mitigate risks. Stakeholders are urged to stay vigilant and report any suspicious activities related to the breach.  
   ↪ <https://www.cuinfosecurity.com/breach-roundup-feds-confirm-major-hack-fbi-system-a-31329>
2. **CVE-2025-55182 Exploited in Breach of 766 Next.js Hosts, Credential Theft** — *tl;dr:* Hackers have exploited CVE-2025-55182, a critical vulnerability in Next.js applications, to compromise 766 hosts and steal sensitive credentials, including AWS secrets and API keys. The operation, attributed to the UAT-10608 threat cluster, utilizes automated scripts to extract data and posts it to a command-and-control server featuring a GUI called 'NEXUS Listener.' Organizations are urged to audit their systems, enforce least privilege, enable secret scanning, and rotate credentials to mitigate risks of further attacks stemming from this breach.  
   ↪ <https://thehackernews.com/2026/04/hackers-exploit-cve-2025-55182-to.html>
3. **Hasbro Investigates Cyberattack Impacting Operations and Potential Data Breach** — *tl;dr:* Hasbro, the renowned toy and board game company, reported a cyberattack on March 28, 2026, disrupting some of its operations. The company has initiated an investigation to assess the extent of the breach and whether any sensitive data was compromised. While business continuity plans are in place, some delays may occur as Hasbro works to restore normal operations. The firm is collaborating with external cybersecurity experts to enhance its security measures and will notify affected parties if necessary. No ransomware group has yet claimed responsibility for the attack.  
   ↪ <https://securityaffairs.com/190306/security/hasbro-hit-by-cyberattack-investigates-possible-data-breach.html>
4. **Elastio Enhances IBM Cloud Object Storage with Ransomware Detection** — *tl;dr:* Elastio introduces deep object inspection for IBM Cloud Object Storage, enabling organizations to detect and recover from ransomware attacks with high precision. This solution addresses the growing threat of ransomware, which often infiltrates systems quietly, making it difficult to identify compromised data. By analyzing file content and structure, Elastio provides definitive insights into data integrity, allowing teams to pinpoint clean data and initiate recovery effectively. As ransomware incidents escalate, businesses must adopt proactive measures like this to safeguard their cloud storage and ensure swift recovery from potential breaches.  
   ↪ <https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ransomware-is-already-in-your-object-storage-prove-what-s-clean-and-recover-with-confidence>
5. **FBI Declares Major Cyber Incident Following Suspected Chinese Hack of Law Enforcement Data** — *tl;dr:* The FBI has classified a suspected Chinese cyber intrusion into a government surveillance system as a 'major incident,' posing significant risks to U.S. national security. The breach compromised sensitive law enforcement data and is linked to tactics used in the Salt Typhoon hacking campaign, which previously accessed telecom systems and FBI wiretap data. This incident highlights ongoing vulnerabilities in U.S. cybersecurity, exacerbated by staffing cuts in federal agencies. Lawmakers, including Sen. Mark Warner, emphasize the need for enhanced cybersecurity measures to protect against such persistent threats from foreign adversaries.  
   ↪ <https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/fbi-labels-suspected-china-hack-law-enforcement-data-major-cyber-incid-rcna266495>
