Artificial Intelligence is not just changing business—it’s rewriting the rules of cybersecurity. As attackers weaponize prompt injection, data poisoning, and model manipulation, we’re witnessing the rise of a **new digital battlefield**.

On **October 9, 2025 (10:00 AM EDT)**, I’ll be speaking at *DevSecOps Live* alongside **Marudhamaran Gunasekaran** to unpack these threats and share real-world insights on how to defend against them.

---

## Why This Matters to Me

Over the past years, I’ve dedicated myself to exploring **AI security vulnerabilities**—not as an academic exercise, but as a **practical mission**.

- I created **AI Risk Inspector**, a tool designed to test, monitor, and secure AI systems.
- I’ve helped organizations rethink how they integrate AI responsibly, exposing blind spots in **traditional cybersecurity approaches**.
- And as a **GenAI Risk Hunter**, I’ve seen firsthand how attackers are already probing and exploiting AI systems.

This talk is an opportunity to **share that journey** with the wider community.

---

## What I’ll Be Covering

🔎 **How attackers target AI systems**: From prompt injection to data poisoning.  
⚔️ **Why AI is an expanded battlefield**: What makes AI security different from IT security.  
📂 **Real-world case studies**: Actual exploits of AI weaknesses.  
🛡 **Defensive strategies**: How to start testing and securing AI deployments today.

This isn’t about fear—it’s about **equipping leaders and teams** with the right mindset and tools to stay ahead.

---

## Why You Should Join

If you’re a CISO, a product security leader, or part of a DevSecOps team, this session is for you. You’ll leave with **actionable strategies** to bring back to your organization and a clearer picture of the **AI security landscape** we’re heading into.
