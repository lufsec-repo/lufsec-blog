## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Ransomware Group nova Targets VX Case in Major Data Breach** — *tl;dr:* On March 29, 2026, VX Case, a Brazilian e-commerce store specializing in mobile accessories, suffered a ransomware attack by the group nova. The breach resulted in the theft of sensitive customer and employee information, including WhatsApp pictures and personal data. Organizations are urged to enhance their cybersecurity measures, such as implementing phishing simulations and data breach monitoring, to mitigate risks. The incident highlights the ongoing threat of ransomware attacks and the necessity for proactive security strategies to protect against potential data leaks.  
   ↪ <https://www.hookphish.com/blog/ransomware-group-nova-hits-vx-case/>
2. **Qilin Ransomware Targets Doctor.com, Exposing Healthcare Data Risks** — *tl;dr:* On March 29, 2026, the Qilin ransomware group announced a cyberattack on Doctor.com, a significant player in the U.S. healthcare sector, threatening to release sensitive data unless negotiations commence. This incident highlights the growing threat of ransomware attacks on healthcare organizations. To mitigate risks, experts recommend continuous monitoring of dark web activities, conducting comprehensive compromise assessments, validating backups, and enhancing employee defenses through phishing simulations and multi-factor authentication. Engaging cybersecurity professionals is crucial before communicating with ransomware actors to ensure proper response protocols are followed.  
   ↪ <https://www.dexpose.io/qilin-ransomware-attack-on-doctor-com-healthcare-data-at-risk/>
3. **European Commission Confirms Data Breach, 350GB Stolen from AWS Infrastructure** — *tl;dr:* The European Commission has confirmed a cyberattack that compromised its AWS-hosted Europa.eu infrastructure, resulting in the theft of approximately 350GB of organizational data. The breach was detected on March 24, 2026, and while the Commission's internal systems remain unaffected, investigations are ongoing to assess the full impact. The attackers, who have not been identified, plan to leak the stolen data online rather than extort the Commission. Affected entities are being notified, and additional security measures are being implemented to safeguard data. Organizations should enhance their cybersecurity protocols to mitigate similar risks.  
   ↪ <https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/european-commission-confirms-platform-data-breach-admits-data-have-been-taken-from-official-websites>
4. **European Commission Confirms Data Breach of Cloud Infrastructure** — *tl;dr:* The European Commission has confirmed a data breach affecting its cloud infrastructure hosting the Europa.eu platform, with hackers claiming to have stolen over 350GB of sensitive data. The breach was detected on March 24, 2026, prompting immediate containment measures. While internal systems remain unaffected, the stolen data reportedly includes personally identifiable information (PII), emails, and confidential documents. The extortion group ShinyHunters is linked to the attack, raising concerns about identity risk and potential spear-phishing attacks. Organizations should enhance their cybersecurity measures and monitor for unusual activities in response to this incident.  
   ↪ <https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/european-commission-cloud-data/>
5. **European Commission Data Breach Linked to ShinyHunters Group** — *tl;dr:* The European Commission has suffered a significant data breach attributed to the notorious hacking group ShinyHunters. This incident has raised concerns over the security of sensitive information within the EU's institutions. Affected parties are urged to assess their data security measures and remain vigilant for any suspicious activities. It is advisable for individuals and organizations to review their cybersecurity protocols and consider implementing additional safeguards to protect against potential future breaches.  
   ↪ <https://cybernews.com/security/european-commission-data-breach-shinyhunters/>
