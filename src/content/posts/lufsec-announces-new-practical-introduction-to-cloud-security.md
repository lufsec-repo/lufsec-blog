LufSec Announces New Practical Introduction to Cloud Security.

Cloud security is a critical requirement for all organizations. Especially with the latest [research from (ISC)2](https://www.isc2.org/Resource-Center/Reports/Cloud-Security-Report) reporting 93% of organizations are moderately or extremely concerned about cloud security, and one in four organizations confirming a cloud security incident in the past 12 months.

The mass adoption of cloud technology combined with an ever-increasing volume and sophistication of cyber threats is what drives the need for cloud security. Reflecting on the security risks of adopting cloud technology – outlined above – failure to mitigate them can come with significant implications.

Be on demand with this new course provided to you by LufSec!
