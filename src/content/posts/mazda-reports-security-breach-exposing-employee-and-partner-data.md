## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Mazda Reports Security Breach Exposing Employee and Partner Data** — *tl;dr:* Mazda Motor Corporation has disclosed a security breach that exposed the personal information of 692 employees and business partners. The breach, detected in December, was due to a vulnerability in a warehouse management system related to parts procured from Thailand, and it did not involve customer data. Exposed information includes user IDs, names, email addresses, company names, and business partner IDs. Although no misuse of the data has been reported, Mazda advises affected individuals to remain vigilant against potential phishing attacks. The company has implemented additional security measures to prevent future incidents.  
   ↪ <https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/mazda-discloses-security-breach-exposing-employee-and-partner-data/>
2. **Citrix Patches Critical NetScaler Vulnerabilities CVE-2026-3055 and CVE-2026-4368** — *tl;dr:* Citrix has released urgent security updates for two vulnerabilities in NetScaler ADC and Gateway, including CVE-2026-3055, which has a CVSS score of 9.3 and allows unauthenticated attackers to leak sensitive data. The second vulnerability, CVE-2026-4368, scores 7.7 and can lead to user session mix-ups. Affected versions include NetScaler ADC and Gateway prior to specified updates. Users are advised to check their configurations and apply the latest patches immediately to mitigate potential exploitation, as similar vulnerabilities have been targeted in the past.  
   ↪ <https://thehackernews.com/2026/03/citrix-urges-patching-critical.html>
3. **Bell Ambulance Data Breach Exposes Data of Over 238,000 Patients** — *tl;dr:* A ransomware attack on Bell Ambulance has compromised the personal and healthcare information of 237,830 individuals. The Medusa ransomware group claimed responsibility, alleging the theft of over 219 gigabytes of sensitive data, including Social Security numbers, medical details, and financial information. The breach occurred between February 7 and February 14, 2025, with the organization notifying affected individuals in March 2026. Bell Ambulance has implemented security measures and is offering twelve months of credit monitoring to those impacted. Affected individuals should monitor their credit reports and financial statements for suspicious activity.  
   ↪ <https://www.paubox.com/blog/bell-ambulance-data-breach-affects-more-than-238000-individuals>
4. **Kaplan Data Breach Exposes Personal Data of Over 230,000 Individuals** — *tl;dr:* Kaplan, a prominent education company, reported a data breach affecting at least 230,000 individuals, with sensitive information including Social Security and driver's license numbers compromised. The breach occurred between October 30 and November 18, 2025, prompting Kaplan to notify state regulators and law enforcement. Affected states include Texas, South Carolina, Maine, and New Hampshire. As class-action lawsuits emerge, individuals are advised to monitor their financial accounts and consider credit monitoring services to mitigate potential identity theft risks.  
   ↪ <https://therecord.media/kaplan-data-breach-hack-notification>
5. **MediCopy Data Breach Exposes Personal and Health Information** — *tl;dr:* MediCopy, a cloud-based file sharing service for healthcare organizations, recently suffered a data breach affecting an unknown number of individuals. The breach occurred when an unauthorized person accessed MediCopy's files via a third-party vendor, potentially compromising personally identifiable information (PII) and protected health information (PHI). Lynch Carpenter, LLP is investigating claims related to this incident. Individuals whose information may have been impacted are encouraged to seek legal advice and may be entitled to compensation.  
   ↪ <https://lynchcarpenter.com/medicopy-data-breach-claims-investigated-by-lynch-carpenter/>
