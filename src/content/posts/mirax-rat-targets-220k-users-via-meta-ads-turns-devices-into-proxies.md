*Tuesday, April 14, 2026*

## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Mirax RAT Targets 220K Users via Meta Ads, Turns Devices into Proxies** — *tl;dr:* The Mirax Android Remote Access Trojan (RAT) has been identified as actively targeting over 220,000 accounts across Meta platforms like Facebook and Instagram, primarily in Spanish-speaking countries. This malware not only allows attackers to control infected devices in real time but also converts them into SOCKS5 proxy nodes, enabling anonymous traffic routing through victims' IP addresses. Users are advised to remain vigilant about suspicious ads and to ensure their devices have updated security measures to mitigate risks associated with such threats.  
   ↪ <https://thehackernews.com/>
2. **Adobe Fixes Actively Exploited Acrobat Reader Vulnerability CVE-2026-34621** — *tl;dr:* Adobe has released emergency updates to address a critical security flaw, CVE-2026-34621, in Acrobat Reader that is actively being exploited. This vulnerability, with a CVSS score of 8.6, allows attackers to execute arbitrary code by manipulating JavaScript in specially crafted PDF documents. Affected products include Acrobat DC and Acrobat Reader DC versions 26.001.21367 and earlier, as well as Acrobat 2024 versions 24.001.30356 and earlier. Users are urged to update to the latest versions immediately to mitigate the risk of exploitation, as the flaw has reportedly been under active attack since December 2025.  
   ↪ <https://thehackernews.com/2026/04/adobe-patches-actively-exploited.html>
3. **Adobe Issues Urgent Fix for Critical Zero-Day Vulnerability CVE-2026-34621** — *tl;dr:* Adobe has released emergency patches for CVE-2026-34621, a critical zero-day vulnerability in Adobe Reader that has reportedly been exploited for months, allowing arbitrary code execution on vulnerable systems. This flaw poses significant risks to enterprises, as PDF readers are common entry points for cyberattacks. Organizations are advised to apply the patches immediately and conduct thorough reviews of document execution artifacts, email delivery paths, and endpoint telemetry to mitigate potential exploitation risks. The urgency of this response highlights the need for proactive vulnerability management in digital environments.  
   ↪ <https://digitalforensicsmagazine.com/news-roundup-13th-april-2026/>
4. **Understanding Data Breaches: Causes, Costs, and Consequences** — *tl;dr:* A data breach occurs when unauthorized parties access sensitive information, such as personal and corporate data. While often confused with cyberattacks, not all cyberattacks result in data breaches. The average cost of a data breach is USD 4.44 million globally, with significant variations across industries and countries. Factors contributing to these costs include lost business, detection, post-breach response, and notification expenses. Organizations must be vigilant against breaches caused by human error, malicious insiders, and hackers. Compliance with regulations like GDPR and HIPAA is crucial for timely breach reporting to mitigate legal repercussions.  
   ↪ <https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/data-breach>
5. **Ransomware Gangs Exploit Four Old Microsoft Vulnerabilities, CISA Warns** — *tl;dr:* The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has identified four Microsoft vulnerabilities being actively exploited by cybercriminals, including one dating back nearly 14 years. The vulnerabilities, which include CVE-2025-60710 and CVE-2023-21529, allow for privilege escalation and remote code execution. CISA has urged federal agencies to patch these flaws by April 27, 2026, highlighting the ongoing risks posed by outdated vulnerabilities. Organizations are advised to review their systems for these CVEs and apply the necessary updates to mitigate potential ransomware attacks.  
   ↪ <https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/13/ransomware_gang_other_crims_attacking/>
