*Thursday, May 7, 2026*

## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Data Breach Alert: Instructure's Canvas Affected, Investigation Ongoing** — *tl;dr:* The University of Nevada, Reno has reported a cybersecurity incident involving Instructure, the provider of the Canvas learning management system. A data breach has been confirmed, potentially exposing names, institutional email addresses, student ID numbers, and private Canvas messages. However, Instructure has stated that sensitive information such as passwords, dates of birth, and financial data remain secure. A forensic investigation is currently underway to determine if the University was affected. Students and staff are advised to be vigilant against suspicious emails and to report any concerning communications to [abuse@unr.edu](mailto:abuse@unr.edu).  
   ↪ <https://www.unr.edu/nevada-today/news/president-messages/2026-05-06-cybersecurity-incident>
2. **Data Breach at Instructure's Canvas Affects All NC Schools' Data** — *tl;dr:* A recent data breach involving Instructure's Canvas learning management system may have compromised student and staff data across all North Carolina public schools. The breach was reported on April 25, 2026, with concerns about the exposure of names, email addresses, and student IDs. While sensitive information like passwords and financial data appears unaffected, cybersecurity experts warn that this data could facilitate phishing attacks. The North Carolina Department of Public Instruction is working to assess the full impact and is advising districts to enhance security measures, including multi-factor authentication.  
   ↪ <https://www.wral.com/news/education/data-breach-wake-county-schools-canvas-powerschool-instructure-may-2026/>
3. **Wake County Schools Reports Canvas Data Breach Exposing Student and Staff Data** — *tl;dr:* The Wake County Public School System has informed parents of a data breach involving the Canvas education software, which may have compromised personal information of students and staff. Although sensitive data like passwords and financial information were reportedly not accessed, concerns about security measures have been raised. This incident follows a previous breach in 2024. Cybersecurity experts advise parents to remain vigilant against phishing attempts, enable multi-factor authentication on accounts, and update passwords. Ongoing investigations urge Canvas users to monitor for suspicious activity.  
   ↪ <https://abc11.com/post/wake-schools-notify-parents-canvas-data-breach/19054909/>
4. **Instructure Data Breach Affects Baylor and Other U.S. Universities** — *tl;dr:* A data breach at Instructure, the provider of the Canvas learning platform, has impacted Baylor University and potentially other higher education institutions in North America. Although Baylor's Bear ID passwords were secure due to the use of DUO two-factor authentication, the breach has caused brief outages on platforms like Respondus and TurnItIn. Instructure is investigating the incident with a forensics firm and advises users to remain vigilant against phishing attempts. Users are encouraged to access online resources directly and report suspicious messages to Baylor's IT department.  
   ↪ <https://its.web.baylor.edu/news/story/2026/instructure-data-breach-impacts-us-universities>
5. **Nationwide Data Breach Affects Canvas Users at UC** — *tl;dr:* Instructure, the provider of the Canvas learning management system, has reported a nationwide data breach impacting numerous institutions, including the University of California. The university is actively collaborating with Instructure and cybersecurity partners to address the situation. Community members are advised to remain vigilant against potential phishing attempts and to be cautious of unexpected communications. The university emphasizes that it will never request sensitive information such as passwords or Social Security numbers via email or phone. Updates will be provided as more information becomes available.  
   ↪ <https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/employee-news/nationwide-security-breach-involving-canvas/>
