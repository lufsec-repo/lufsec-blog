*Tuesday, May 12, 2026*

## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **New TrickMo Variant Targets Banking Users with Enhanced C2 Capabilities** — *tl;dr:* A new variant of the TrickMo Android banking trojan has been identified, utilizing The Open Network (TON) for command-and-control (C2) operations. This malware, active since late 2019, has been observed targeting banking and cryptocurrency wallet users in France, Italy, and Austria. Key features include reconnaissance, SSH tunneling, and SOCKS5 proxying, enabling infected devices to act as network pivots. Users are advised to ensure their devices are updated and to exercise caution when downloading apps, particularly from untrusted sources, to mitigate the risk of infection.  
   ↪ <https://thehackernews.com/>
2. **State-Sponsored Hackers Exploit Palo Alto Firewall Zero-Day CVE-2026-0300** — *tl;dr:* State-backed hackers are exploiting a critical zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2026-0300) in Palo Alto Networks firewalls, allowing remote code execution without authentication. The flaw, linked to the Captive Portal feature in PAN-OS, has a CVSS severity rating of 9.3. Attacks began on April 9, with successful exploitation leading to unauthorized access and deeper network infiltration. Palo Alto advises customers to restrict access to the User-ID Authentication Portal or disable it entirely until a patch is released. The situation underscores ongoing risks for PAN-OS users, who have faced multiple zero-day threats recently.  
   ↪ <https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/05/07/state-backed-hackers-hammer-palo-alto-firewall-zero-day-before-patch-lands/5234737>
3. **Critical Linux Zero-Day 'Dirty Frag' Disclosed After Nine Years; Ivanti CVE-2026-6973 Exploited** — *tl;dr:* A newly disclosed Linux zero-day vulnerability, nicknamed 'Dirty Frag,' has been present for nearly nine years, allowing local attackers to escalate privileges to root across major distributions like Ubuntu and Red Hat. At the same time, Ivanti revealed CVE-2026-6973, a high-severity remote code execution flaw in their Endpoint Manager Mobile product, actively exploited in the wild. Users are urged to apply available patches immediately, as both vulnerabilities pose significant risks to system security. The situation highlights the urgent need for timely software updates and awareness of ongoing threats.  
   ↪ <https://medium.com/@ddmatiq762/zero-day-vulnerabilities-the-hackers-secret-weapon-bc321bae5548>
4. **Canvas Data Leak Threatens 8,800 Schools Amid Ransom Demand** — *tl;dr:* Attackers linked to The Com are threatening to leak sensitive data from over 8,800 school systems unless Instructure pays a ransom. This data breach raises significant concerns about the security of educational institutions and the potential exposure of student information. Schools must assess their cybersecurity measures and consider implementing stronger data protection protocols to mitigate risks. Stakeholders in the education sector should stay informed about the situation and prepare for possible fallout from the breach.  
   ↪ <https://cyberscoop.com/>
5. **JDownloader Site Hacked to Distribute Python RAT Malware** — *tl;dr:* The JDownloader website was recently compromised, leading to the distribution of malicious installers for both Windows and Linux users. The attack involved a Python-based remote access trojan (RAT) embedded in the Windows installer, posing significant risks to users who downloaded the compromised software. This incident highlights the importance of downloading software from official sources and maintaining up-to-date security measures. Users are advised to verify the integrity of their downloads and consider using antivirus tools to detect potential threats.  
   ↪ <https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/>
