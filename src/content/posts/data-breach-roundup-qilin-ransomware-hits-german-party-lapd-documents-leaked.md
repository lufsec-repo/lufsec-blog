*Monday, April 13, 2026*

## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Data Breach Roundup: Qilin Ransomware Hits German Party, LAPD Documents Leaked** — *tl;dr:* During the week of April 3-9, 2026, significant data breaches were reported, including an attack on the German political party Die Linke by the Qilin ransomware group, which claims to have stolen employee data despite the party asserting that its membership database was secure. Additionally, Mercor, a $10 billion AI startup, confirmed a major breach linked to the LiteLLM supply chain attack, with leaked datasets and internal communications. In a separate incident, hackers leaked sensitive LAPD documents, including personnel files and unredacted criminal complaints, totaling 7.7 TB. Affected organizations should enhance their cybersecurity measures and conduct thorough investigations.  
   ↪ <https://www.privacyguides.org/news/2026/04/12/data-breach-roundup-apr-3-9-2026/>
2. **Ransomware Groups Standardize Double Extortion Tactics with AI Tools, Cyfirma Reports** — *tl;dr:* Cyfirma's latest report reveals that ransomware groups are increasingly adopting standardized double extortion tactics, combining data encryption with theft to maximize pressure on victims. The report highlights a shift towards AI-assisted targeting and automation, allowing smaller threat actors to scale operations efficiently. Key sectors affected include manufacturing, healthcare, and IT, with the U.S. remaining the primary target. As ransom demands rise, organizations must enhance their cybersecurity measures, focusing on rapid vulnerability management and employee training to mitigate risks associated with these evolving ransomware strategies.  
   ↪ <https://industrialcyber.co/ransomware/ransomware-groups-standardize-double-extortion-and-ai-assisted-targeting-cyfirma-reports/>
3. **1,615 Vulnerabilities Identified in April 6-12, 2026 CVE Briefing** — *tl;dr:* During the week of April 6-12, 2026, a total of 1,615 vulnerabilities were reported, with over 40% classified as High or Critical severity. Notable issues include Adobe's urgent patch for an actively exploited flaw in Acrobat Reader (CVE-2026-34621) and security advisories from TP-Link regarding five high-severity flaws in its routers. Organizations are advised to prioritize patching these vulnerabilities to mitigate potential risks. Additionally, OpenAI has warned macOS users to update ChatGPT due to a security issue linked to the Axios library. Vigilance and prompt action are essential for maintaining cybersecurity.  
   ↪ <https://infosecbulletin.com/infosecbulletin-weekly-cve-briefing-april-6-april-12-2026/>
4. **Rockstar Games Hacked Again by ShinyHunters, Downplays Data Breach Impact** — *tl;dr:* Rockstar Games, the developer of Grand Theft Auto, has suffered a data breach for the second time in three years, attributed to the hacker group ShinyHunters. They claimed to have accessed Rockstar's servers via a third-party cloud provider and threatened to publish stolen data unless a ransom was paid. However, Rockstar downplayed the incident, stating that only a limited amount of non-material company information was accessed, and assured players that there was no impact on their experience. Cybersecurity experts advise against paying ransoms, as it may not guarantee the return of stolen data.  
   ↪ <https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2dg5g1le7o>
5. **EDR Bypass Technique Exploited: BYOVD Approach to Terminate SentinelOne** — *tl;dr:* Joseph Wimalasuriya discusses the use of the BYOVD (Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver) technique to bypass Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) solutions, specifically targeting SentinelOne. The method involves terminating SentinelOne processes without detection due to the use of a signed driver. This raises concerns about the effectiveness of current EDR systems against sophisticated cyberattacks. Security professionals are advised to enhance their detection capabilities and consider more discreet methods of threat neutralization to avoid triggering alerts in Security Operations Centers (SOCs).  
   ↪ <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/joseph-wimalasuriya-473b97198_cybersecurity-cyberattack-cybercrime-activity-7449328287954210817-DxsH>
