## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **ADT Data Breach Exposes Customer Personal Information** — *tl;dr:* ADT has confirmed a data breach affecting its current and prospective customers, with personal information such as names, phone numbers, and addresses accessed. While the company reported that no payment information was compromised, a small percentage of cases included dates of birth and partial Social Security numbers. ADT is notifying affected individuals and will provide free identity protection services. The extent of the breach remains unclear, with reports suggesting that up to 10 million records may have been stolen. Customers are advised to monitor their accounts closely for any suspicious activity.  
   ↪ <https://www.ktvu.com/news/adt-confirms-data-breach-customer-personal-information-accessed>
2. **ADT Confirms Data Breach Exposing Customer Personal Information** — *tl;dr:* ADT has confirmed a data breach affecting both current and prospective customers, revealing personal information such as names, phone numbers, and addresses. The breach, detected by ADT's cybersecurity team, did not compromise payment information or security systems. While the company has notified those affected and plans to offer free identity protection, the extent of the data stolen remains unclear, with reports suggesting up to 10 million records may be involved. Customers are advised to monitor their accounts and consider utilizing the identity protection services offered by ADT.  
   ↪ <https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/adt-confirms-data-breach-customer-140825119.html>
3. **Itron Reports Cyberattack on Internal IT Network, No Customer Data Compromised** — *tl;dr:* Itron, a utility technology company, has disclosed a cyberattack that allowed unauthorized access to its internal IT systems. The breach was detected on April 13, 2026, prompting Itron to activate its cybersecurity response plan and engage external advisors for investigation and containment. Fortunately, the company reported no material disruption to its operations, and customer data remains secure. While Itron expects incident-related costs to be largely covered by insurance, the investigation into the breach's full scope is ongoing. Companies in critical infrastructure sectors should review their cybersecurity measures to prevent similar incidents.  
   ↪ <https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/american-utility-firm-itron-discloses-breach-of-internal-it-network/>
4. **Udemy Data Breach: ShinyHunters Claims Compromise of 1.4 Million Accounts** — *tl;dr:* A recent data breach involving Udemy has been claimed by the hacker group ShinyHunters, who allege that they have compromised 1.4 million user accounts. This incident raises concerns about the security of user data, as personal information may be at risk. Affected users are advised to change their passwords immediately and enable two-factor authentication where possible to enhance their account security. Udemy has not yet confirmed the breach, but users should remain vigilant for any suspicious activity related to their accounts.  
   ↪ <https://x.com/The_Cyber_News/status/2048594951316124002>
5. **Ransomware Attacks Hit Legend Senior Living and Winward Life Care** — *tl;dr:* Legend Senior Living and Winward Life Care recently confirmed ransomware attacks affecting their networks. Legend reported unauthorized access to sensitive personal and health information of over 5,000 Texas residents, with the Worldleaks group claiming responsibility. Meanwhile, Winward Life Care experienced a breach on December 8, where a ransomware group, Sinobi, accessed and encrypted files, exfiltrating 25 gigabytes of data. Both organizations urge affected individuals to monitor their accounts and consider identity theft protection services following these incidents.  
   ↪ <https://www.mcknightsseniorliving.com/news/legend-winward-ransomware-attacks/>
