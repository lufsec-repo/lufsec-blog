## Top 5 Cybersecurity Stories You Should Know

1. **Citizens and Frost Blame Vendor for Data Breach Linked to Everest Ransomware Group** — *tl;dr:* Citizens Bank and Frost Bank are attributing a recent data breach to a compromised third-party vendor, following claims from the Everest ransomware group that they stole millions of records. Citizens reported that the stolen data primarily consists of masked test data, while Frost acknowledged potential unauthorized access to customer data. Both banks have engaged cybersecurity experts and found no evidence of breaches within their own networks. The incident highlights the risks associated with outsourcing to vendors and underscores the importance of robust vendor management and cybersecurity measures.  
   ↪ <https://www.americanbanker.com/news/citizens-frost-blame-vendor-after-data-breach-claim>
2. **ZeroDay Pvt. Ltd. Warns of Daily Data Breaches: Secure Your Assets Now** — *tl;dr:* ZeroDay Pvt. Ltd. has issued a live data breach alert, emphasizing the increasing frequency of data breaches that threaten organizations daily. These breaches can lead to unauthorized access to sensitive data, financial fraud, business disruptions, and a loss of customer trust. To combat these threats, ZeroDay offers services such as real-time vulnerability assessments, continuous security monitoring, and proactive threat intelligence. Organizations are urged to take action now to secure their digital assets before attackers exploit vulnerabilities. For more information, visit their website or contact them directly.  
   ↪ <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/0zero-day_cybersecurity-databreach-infosec-activity-7452987617802932224-bDnm>
3. **CVE-2026-33825: Microsoft Defender Zero-Day Exploited for Privilege Escalation** — *tl;dr:* A recently disclosed vulnerability in Microsoft Defender, tracked as CVE-2026-33825, has been exploited in the wild as a zero-day, allowing attackers to access the Security Account Manager (SAM) database and gain System privileges. The flaw, named BlueHammer, stems from insufficient access control and was publicly disclosed by researcher Chaotic Eclipse. Cybersecurity firm Huntress reported that the first attacks began shortly after the vulnerability was made public. Organizations using Microsoft Defender are urged to apply the patch released on April 14, 2026, to mitigate potential exploitation before the May 6 deadline set by CISA.  
   ↪ <https://www.securityweek.com/recent-microsoft-defender-vulnerability-exploited-as-zero-day/>
4. **Data Breach at France's ANTS Exposes 19 Million Records** — *tl;dr:* France's national agency for managing IDs and passports, Agence nationale des titres sécurisés (ANTS), confirmed a data breach that occurred on April 15, 2026. A hacker claims to have obtained up to 19 million records, including full names, email addresses, dates of birth, and other personal information. While the breach did not allow access to agency portals, the exposed data poses a risk for phishing attacks and other illicit activities. Affected individuals are advised to remain vigilant against suspicious communications and take necessary precautions to protect their personal information.  
   ↪ <https://www.engadget.com/cybersecurity/frances-national-agency-for-managing-ids-and-passports-suffered-a-data-breach-last-week-201432189.html>
5. **Data Breach at France's ANTS Exposes Citizens' Personal Information** — *tl;dr:* The Agence Nationale des Titres Sécurisés (ANTS), responsible for managing French citizens' identity documents, has confirmed a significant data breach affecting potentially millions of individuals. The stolen data may include full names, birth dates, addresses, and phone numbers. ANTS detected the breach on April 15 and is currently investigating its origins and impact while notifying affected citizens. A hacker has claimed to possess a database with 19 million records related to this breach, raising concerns about identity theft and privacy. Citizens are advised to monitor their personal information and report any suspicious activity.  
   ↪ <https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/22/france-confirms-data-breach-at-government-agency-that-manages-citizens-ids/>
