Imagine tapping into the minds of some of the world's leading cloud security experts. The dream is about to become a reality as the second edition of the SANS Cloud Security Exchange is set to take place. Last year's event was a monumental success, and 2023 is geared up to be even better!

## Why Should You Attend?

Cloud security is a top priority for every business with a presence on the cloud. With cyber threats becoming more sophisticated, it’s crucial to stay ahead of the curve. The Cloud Security Exchange 2023 is a gold mine for anyone looking to fortify their cloud infrastructure. With industry leaders from AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and the SANS Institute sharing the stage, this virtual event is the ultimate cloud security knowledge hub.

## When & Where?

- **Date**: Friday, 18th August 2023
- **Time**: 11 am EDT
- **Where**: Online - Yes, that's right! You can absorb all this knowledge from the comfort of your home or office!

## Who Are The Experts?

Dr. Anton Chuvakin from Google Cloud is one of the many luminaries who will grace the event. He will open the event by discussing the shared responsibility of cloud security and how Artificial Intelligence is revolutionizing cybersecurity. Dr. Chuvakin is renowned for his expertise, and his session is bound to provide some invaluable insights.

That's not all; Dr. Andre Alfred from Microsoft Azure will take the stage next. He will reveal Microsoft’s strategies for protecting its cloud infrastructure and how you can adopt the same defenses!

Moreover, the experienced instructors from SANS Institute, including Simon Vernon, Brandon Evans, and Megan Roddie, will debunk myths holding organizations back from embracing cloud technologies.

AWS is not staying behind; Mark Ryland, Director, Office of the CISO at Amazon Web Services, will also share his expertise, which you do not want to miss.

Towards the end, an entourage of SANS instructors will join the event to share their vast experiences, discussing what strategies have proven successful and what to avoid in the ever-evolving landscape of cloud security.
