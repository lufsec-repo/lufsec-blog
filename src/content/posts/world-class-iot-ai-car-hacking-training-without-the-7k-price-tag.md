I don’t teach theory for the sake of theory. These courses are lab-first, research-driven, and globally tested. I’ve participated in trainings and conferences around the world, from Black Hat USA to DEF CON workshops, and distilled the best content into practical modules you can use immediately.

Here’s what makes them different:

- Hands-On Labs: Real-world simulations, not endless slides.
- Global Research & Experience: Built from the same material I’ve trained and studied worldwide.
- Practical Takeaways: Skills you can apply on the job or in CTFs.
- Affordable Access: The same level of training—without the corporate price tag.

**The Courses**

**🔹** [**IoT Hacking, Pentesting, and Exploitation**](https://www.lufsec.com/products/courses/iot-hacking) **— $299 ($149 with LUFSEC50)**

Explore interfaces in IoT devices and develop practical skills to evaluate and secure them. From firmware analysis to UART access, fuzzing, and exploitation, this course gives you the tools to handle IoT security in industries ranging from automotive to healthcare.

👉 You’ll learn how to: extract firmware, exploit real-world flaws, and design repeatable test strategies.

**🔹** [**AI Hacking: Secure Large Language Models**](https://www.lufsec.com/products/courses/ai-hacking) **— $299 ($149 with LUFSEC50)**

Master ethical hacking of AI systems. Learn prompt exploitation, contextual attacks, and practical defenses to make AI applications resilient. With adversarial examples, input sanitization, and runtime guardrails, this course puts you on the frontier of AI security.

👉 You’ll learn how to: evaluate model robustness, simulate adversarial threats, and secure production systems.

**🔹** [**Car Hacking 101: From Key Fob Cracking to Full System Exploitation**](https://www.lufsec.com/products/courses/car-hacking)**our move to the new platform, I’m offering — $299 ($149 with LUFSEC50)**

👉 You’ll learn how to: analyze ECUs, run replay/fuzzing attacks, and propose mitigations for modern vehicles.
