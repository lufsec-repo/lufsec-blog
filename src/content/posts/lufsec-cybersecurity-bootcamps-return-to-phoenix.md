#### **Introduction**

Exciting news for all cybersecurity enthusiasts! LufSec CyberSecurity Bootcamps are back in Phoenix with three exclusive events designed to boost your skills and career prospects. Whether you’re diving into IoT hacking, mastering web application security, or preparing for CISM certification, our boot camps provide hands-on training guided by industry experts. Don’t miss your chance to learn cutting-edge techniques and connect with other professionals in the field.

##### **1. IoT Hacking CyberSecurity Bootcamp (September 28-29, 2024)**

Securing connected devices is crucial in the rapidly evolving world of IoT. Our CyberSecurity Bootcamp in Phoenix is tailored for those who want to dive deep into offensive security techniques specifically for IoT systems. This course covers practical hacking skills that will allow you to identify and exploit vulnerabilities in IoT devices. You’ll work directly with various IoT platforms and tools, giving you the hands-on experience to tackle today’s cybersecurity threats.

**Why Attend?**

- **Hands-on Learning:** Directly engage with IoT hardware and software for a fully immersive experience.
- **Expert Guidance:** Learn from seasoned professionals with industry knowledge.
- **Career Boost:** Equip yourself with highly sought-after skills in the cybersecurity market.

##### **2. Web Application Hacking Bootcamp (October 26-27, 2024)**

Web applications are often the front line of defense for many organizations. This boot camp focuses on web application penetration testing, teaching you how to identify common vulnerabilities, such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and broken authentication. You will learn how to perform comprehensive security assessments on web applications using industry-standard tools and techniques, making this course ideal for security professionals looking to deepen their knowledge.

**What You Will Learn:**

- **Advanced Testing Techniques:** Understand the latest methodologies for assessing web applications.
- **Real-World Scenarios:** Engage in simulations that mimic actual attack environments.
- **Professional Tools:** Get hands-on with Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP, and more.

##### **3. CISM Certification Bootcamp (November 16-17, 2024)**

Our CISM (Certified Information Security Manager) Bootcamp is the perfect pathway for those looking to step into a managerial role. This intensive course will equip you with the skills and knowledge to manage, design, and assess an enterprise’s information security program. Earning a CISM certification will not only validate your expertise but also open doors to higher-level positions within the cybersecurity field.

**Key Highlights:**

- **Focused Preparation:** Comprehensive coverage of all four domains of CISM.
- **Interactive Sessions:** Engage in discussions and Q&A sessions with instructors to deepen understanding.
- **Certification Ready:** Leave the boot camp with the confidence and skills to pass the CISM exam.
