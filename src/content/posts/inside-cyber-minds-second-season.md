Inside Cyber Minds, the cybersecurity podcast from LufSec, is returning for its second season with a new lineup of high-impact conversations featuring some of the most recognized voices in cyber conflict, hacktivism, cloud security, vulnerability disclosure, AI security, privacy, and cybersecurity leadership.

Starting June 10 at 4 PM, a new episode will be released every Wednesday through July 29.

This season is built around one idea: go beyond the resume and understand how influential cybersecurity professionals think.

## What Is Inside Cyber Minds?

Inside Cyber Minds is a cybersecurity podcast created by LufSec that explores the stories, decisions, lessons, and perspectives of the people shaping the cybersecurity industry.

The podcast is not designed to be another surface-level interview show. The goal is to have deeper conversations about the moments, risks, conflicts, and hard-earned lessons that define a career in cybersecurity.

Each episode focuses on the guest’s journey, expertise, mindset, and view of where the industry is going next.

## When Does Inside Cyber Minds Second Season Start?

Inside Cyber Minds, Second Season starts on June 10 at 4 PM.

New episodes will be published weekly every Wednesday until July 29, completing an eight-episode season focused on different areas of cybersecurity.

## Inside Cyber Minds Second Season Guest Lineup

The second season will feature eight guests from different areas of the cybersecurity world.

### Dmitri Alperovitch

Dmitri Alperovitch opens the season on June 10. The conversation will explore cyber conflict, national security, geopolitics, and how cybersecurity has become connected to global power, intelligence, and strategy.

### Hector Monsegur, also known as Sabu

Hector Monsegur, also known as Sabu, brings one of the most unique perspectives in cybersecurity. As a former figure associated with Anonymous and LulzSec, his story connects hacktivism, cybercrime, law enforcement, personal transformation, and the consequences of actions in the digital world.

### Marcio Cots

Marcio Cots brings a perspective focused on privacy, law, digital regulation, and the growing intersection between cybersecurity and legal risk. As companies face more regulatory pressure, these topics are becoming essential for cybersecurity leaders.

### Toni de la Fuente

Toni de la Fuente, founder of Prowler, brings a cloud security and open-source security perspective. His episode will be especially relevant for security teams dealing with cloud infrastructure, automation, misconfiguration risk, and modern security operations.

### JP Calabio

JP Calabio joins the season to discuss cybersecurity leadership, career growth, professional development, and the human side of building a meaningful path in security.

### David Garvin

David Garvin brings experience and perspective around cybersecurity leadership, risk, and the realities of working in the field. His episode will explore lessons learned from practical security work and decision-making.

### Roger Grimes

Roger Grimes from KnowBe4 joins the season to discuss security awareness, social engineering, phishing, identity, and the human factors that continue to shape cybersecurity risk.

### Katie Moussouris

Katie Moussouris from Luta Security brings deep expertise in vulnerability disclosure, bug bounty programs, vulnerability coordination, and security policy. Her episode will explore how organizations should think about working with researchers and managing vulnerability risk responsibly.

## Why This Season Matters

Cybersecurity is no longer limited to technical controls, malware analysis, or compliance checklists.

Today, cybersecurity is connected to national security, business resilience, privacy, AI, cloud infrastructure, cybercrime, vulnerability research, and leadership. The people working in this industry are making decisions that affect companies, governments, and individuals.

Inside Cyber Minds, Second Season, is designed to help listeners understand those decisions from the inside.

The season will explore questions such as:

- What does cyber conflict look like in the real world?
- How do former hackers think about consequences, accountability, and transformation?
- What does the cybersecurity industry still misunderstand about vulnerability disclosure?
- How should organizations approach cloud security at scale?
- Why is people one of the hardest parts of cybersecurity?
- What should the next generation of cybersecurity professionals learn now?
